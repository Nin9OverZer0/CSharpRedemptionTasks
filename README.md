# 3D Buzz C# Redemption Tasks

This repository contains the rules and instructions for completing the **3D Buzz C# 101 + C# 102** tutorials as a condition for programming lessons to resume.

These tasks are available to my nephews (and my sister if she wants to follow along).

---

## What You Must Do

You must complete **all 13 tutorials** from the 3D Buzz C# series:

- **C# 101**
- **C# 102**

### About the Tutorials

- These are console application tutorials.
- Each one commonly runs around **4 hours**.
- They were recorded live with a real class, so students ask questions during the lessons.
- Many of those questions will be ones you would ask yourself.
- Every minute is worth listening to - they explain how programming actually works in a clear and practical way.
- If you want to properly understand programming, these are excellent people to learn from.

### Rules

1. You must do the work **yourself**, in your own time.
2. I will **not** sit with you or do the tutorials with you.
3. There will be **homework** after (or as part of) the tutorials.
4. I will mark the homework and review your code.
5. I will tell you what is good, what is bad, what is correct, and what is incorrect.
6. I will **not** give you the answers.
7. You are expected to learn how to look things up (Google, Stack Overflow, official documentation, etc.).
8. You may only ask me to look at a later lesson **after** you have completed and submitted the previous lesson(s).
